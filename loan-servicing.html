Services · Loan Servicing

Full-service loan servicing for investors

We offer investors a complete menu of services to manage their loan portfolio — from performing loans to full-scale loss mitigation, at a fixed, predictable cost per loan.

Our services

Whatever stage your loan is in, we've got it covered

Specialty in junior liens, with experience managing risk and working directly with borrowers.

1Performing Loan Servicing

We manage your performing loan portfolio with straightforward per-loan pricing, providing predictable monthly and annual servicing costs based on the number of loans we service.

2Wrap Loan Servicing

We manage your performing wrap loan portfolio with straightforward per-loan pricing, providing predictable monthly and annual servicing costs based on the number of loans we service.

3Escrow Services

We handle escrow services for your loans by collecting the monthly impounds to pay taxes and insurance on behalf of the borrower.

4Non-Performing Loan Servicing

We handle non-performing loans — making out-bound collection calls, mailing demand letters and home owner option letters per investor guidelines, and hiring door knockers and occupancy inspection services at a fixed cost per loan.

5Full Service Loss Mitigation

Our borrower intervention program includes skip-tracing, outbound calls by a real person, express/priority/certified mailings, and a "boots on the ground" strategy to build a win-win plan for the borrower and investor.

6Foreclosure Management

We've built relationships with foreclosure attorneys nationwide, vetted for value, expediency, and excellence.

7REO Management

We have relationships with property managers and property management resources across the country when needed.

8Web Portal Access

View your loans on the Madison web portal — payment history, servicing notes, loan balance, last payment date, next due date, loan status, and payoff information, all at no additional cost.
