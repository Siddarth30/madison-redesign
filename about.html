About Us

Founded by investors, built for people

Madison Management Services, LLC is a private mortgage company focused on servicing loans for private investors — and on preserving home ownership for the borrowers behind every loan.

Our history

Madison was founded by Kevin Cordell and Sadhna Cordell on October 9, 2007. As real estate and note investors themselves, they created Madison to serve the needs of other investors — and to this day, Madison offers professional and personalized attention to each of its investor-clients.

Even though Madison has grown significantly since its early days, we still operate by the same core principles we started with.

Our mission

Madison focuses on helping to preserve home ownership through working with our clients and their borrowers to develop creative solutions for each homeowner. Our professional team works individually with each homeowner to develop a plan for their specific situation that results in a win-win solution.

Guiding principle

Create value for investors and consumers while preserving home ownership. We support an environment of the highest standards, fostering ethical personal and business practices.

We always remember that when we deal with borrowers, there is a person on the other end of the phone — and if we can connect with them in a compassionate, transparent way, we get better results for them and for the note-holder.
