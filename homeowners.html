Services · Homeowners

Past due or facing foreclosure? There's a path forward.

Madison Management's Home Owner Assistance Team may be able to work out a way for you to catch up or minimize the damage to your credit. Eligibility is determined case-by-case at (877) 563-4164.

Possible solutions

Options we may be able to offer you

Every situation is different — here's what a workout plan can look like.

1Forbearance Plans

A written agreement to reduce or suspend payments due on your loan for a limited, specific time period. During forbearance, Madison will hold off on initiating foreclosure action. A forbearance plan is often followed by reinstating the loan via a lump sum, reinstatement plan, repayment plan, loan modification, or short sale.

2Repayment Plans

You agree to pay your current monthly payment plus an additional amount, plus a down payment, to repay the outstanding debt owed over an established time period. Completing the plan brings your loan current.

3Loan Modifications

A structured agreement where one or more terms of your mortgage are changed to enable you to bring your defaulted loan current.

4Refinance

Madison works closely with lenders who might be able to help you refinance your loan — in certain circumstances the current owner of your mortgage may help with closing costs, accept a separate note for debt that can't be included in the refinance, or forgive some debt to facilitate it.

5Deed in Lieu

You agree to sign over your property to Madison Management Services in exchange for Madison not foreclosing on your home.

6Short Sales

Occurs when you sell your property and the proceeds are less than the total amount owed. Madison may be able to approve your short sale and forgive the remaining debt owed.

Additional resources

Outside help, if you want a second opinion

These national, non-profit resources are independent of Madison and can offer free guidance.

1HUD-Approved Counseling

The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development sponsors housing counseling agencies to help homeowners avoid losing their homes. Call (800) 569-4287 or visit www.hud.gov.

2Home Learning Center

Operated by the Mortgage Bankers Association, HomeLearningCenter.com provides tips for homeowners and a Foreclosure Prevention Center for borrowers struggling with payments.

3NFCC

The National Foundation for Credit Counseling refers borrowers to NFCC-certified credit counselors, including a free Mortgage Reality Check quiz to see how your mortgage fits your budget.

4Hope Now

An alliance of counselors, mortgage companies, and investors working together to reach and help as many distressed homeowners as possible stay in their homes.

Escrow accounts

How we handle your taxes & insurance

Property taxes

If you have an escrow account, we'll arrange with your tax authority to have your regular tax bills sent directly to us — though some authorities may continue sending bills to you directly. Corrected, supplemental, or special assessment bills are always sent directly to you. If you receive a tax bill and have an escrow account, write your loan number on it and send it to our Tax Department at 4600 Kietzke Ln, Ste K-225, Reno, NV 89502.

Homeowners & hazard insurance

Your mortgage requires you to maintain a property insurance policy with appropriate coverage, including windstorm and earthquake coverage if required, and flood insurance if you're in a FEMA-designated flood zone. If you have an escrow account, annual premiums are paid from it automatically. If you don't, you must provide us a copy of your current policy and payment receipt — otherwise we'll obtain coverage at your expense, which may not be comparable to a policy you'd choose yourself.

Insurance questions: (877) 563-4164 · Fax (877) 563-4161.

Complaints & state notices

We're here to help

If you're having a problem with any part of your mortgage experience, we want to make it right.

Filing a formal complaint

Call Customer Service at (877) 563-4164 for prompt, personalized assistance. To file a formal complaint, notice of error, or qualified written request, mail a letter to: Madison Management Services, LLC, Attn: Customer Requests, 4600 Kietzke Ln, Ste K-225, Reno, NV 89502.

California, Nevada, South Carolina & Washington residents

Madison offers loan modifications and repayment plans to help homeowners struggling with mortgage payments. Call 833-685-2565 (Mon–Fri, 6am–5pm PT) to speak with a Madison specialist, or visit HUD.gov for approved counseling agencies.

Texas residents

Consumers wishing to file a complaint against a mortgage banker or licensed residential mortgage loan originator should contact the Texas Department of Savings and Mortgage Lending, 2601 North Lamar, Suite 201, Austin, TX 78705, or call the consumer hotline at 1-877-276-5550. Complaint forms are available at www.sml.texas.gov.

New Jersey & Illinois residents

New Jersey law prohibits discrimination in housing. Illinois' Department of Financial and Professional Regulation evaluates our performance in meeting the financial services needs of the community, including low-to-moderate-income households, under the state's Community Reinvestment Notice.
