Services · Investors

A servicing company for investors, by investors

Madison Management Services, LLC provides servicing, subservicing, asset management, loss mitigation, and REO management for investors of residential mortgages — headquartered in Reno, Nevada, servicing 47 states, Washington DC, and Puerto Rico.

Founded by an investor, for investors

Madison was founded by Kevin Cordell, a veteran real estate and note investor. As an investor himself, Kevin created Madison to serve the needs of other investors. To this day, Madison offers professional, personalized attention to each of its investor-clients.

What we offer

  • Working with borrowers to develop win-win solutions within investor guidelines.
  • A single point of contact for borrowers.
  • No charge to borrowers or investors for ACH payment processing.
  • Management of borrower impound accounts (taxes and/or insurance).
  • Monthly statements mailed to borrowers with payment coupon.

Our mission

Specializing in servicing and loss mitigation for non-performing loans, Madison provides professional loan servicing, asset management, loss mitigation, and REO management to holders of residential mortgages. Every loan is analyzed strategically, evaluated individually, and handled professionally — guided entirely by the individual investor's or lender's goals.

Before you begin

Madison Management Services will not service the following types of loans: negative amortization, daily simple interest loans (interest accrual method from date received to date received), or any notes that charge interest on interest or interest on late fees.

  • A complete Loan Servicing Setup must be received to start servicing.
  • The Setup Fee is due at the time of setup, with all documents/information, and is non-refundable.
  • Missing documents/information requested by Madison must be provided within 30 days from initial boarding, or the loan will be deboarded.
  • Loans can be prelim-boarded with copies of unrecorded documents, but copies of required recorded documents (deed, mortgage, assignments, etc.) must be provided within 60 days or the loan will be deboarded.
