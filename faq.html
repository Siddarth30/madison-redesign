Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common accounting and servicing questions. Don't see yours? Call (877) 563-4164 or visit our Contact page.

Accounting FAQs

Common questions from investors

Why are you not collecting late fees from the borrower?▾

If the borrower only sent in enough funds to make a PI or PITI payment, the late fee will be assessed and accrue on the loan until the late balance is paid. Payments are applied in order: interest due, principal due, and escrow amounts. Additional funds are applied first to late charges, second to other amounts due (legal fees, other expenses), and last to the principal balance.

Why was my borrower's payment placed in reserve?▾

Borrower payments that are less than the monthly PI will be placed in reserve until funds are received to make a complete monthly payment. A payment in excess of two monthly payments will also be put into reserve.

When will the loan status change to performing?▾

Borrowers must make payments for three (3) consecutive months before a loan is categorized from "non-performing" to "performing." Loans are updated automatically each month based on payment history — two consecutive missed payments switch a loan to "non-performing," and three consecutive payments switch it back to "performing."

Can I endorse a check to Madison Management Services?▾

Yes — you can endorse the back of money orders and checks to Madison Management Services. The back must read "Pay to the order of Madison Management Services, LLC" and be signed by you.

I received funds directly from my borrower. Can the account be updated to reflect these payments?▾

No. Madison must receive funds directly to credit the borrower's account, for accounting and auditing purposes, and to accurately report to the credit bureau and generate year-end tax forms.

How can I pay my invoices?▾

Invoices are deducted from your half-monthly borrower collections check. We also offer auto-draft payments via ACH — funds are debited once a month during the first week for any open invoices. Checks can be mailed to Madison Management Services, LLC, 4600 Kietzke Lane, Ste K-225, Reno, NV 89502. We're unable to accept debit or credit card payment at this time.

When will I receive my invoices?▾

Invoices are generated on the 15th of every month. You'll receive an email once invoices are uploaded to your Sharefile account.

Homeowner FAQs

Common questions from homeowners

What can I do if I'm already in foreclosure or received a foreclosure notice?▾

Contact your Customer Service Representative at (877) 563-4164 immediately. Even if you feel you have no resources or ability to repay your loan, your representative may be able to help you work something out to save your home or, at the very least, minimize the damage to your credit. Doing nothing guarantees the worst possible outcome.

Can I sell my home if I owe more than it's worth?▾

In some cases, yes — this is known as a short sale or pre-foreclosure sale, and it's something you'd work out with your lender. Contact your Customer Service Representative at (877) 563-4164 or customerservice@madisonmanagement.net.

What will a foreclosure do to my credit rating?▾

Foreclosures are extremely damaging to credit and may stay on your report for up to seven years, making it harder to get a future home loan, college financing, or a credit card — and any credit you do get will likely carry a higher rate. Even if you're already facing foreclosure, there may still be options to minimize the damage. Call (877) 563-4164 or email customerservice@madisonmanagement.net.

What if I can't make my payment?▾

Contact us immediately at (877) 563-4164 or customerservice@madisonmanagement.net. Even if you don't have the resources to repay your loan, your representative may be able to help you work something out to save your home or minimize the damage to your credit. Doing nothing guarantees the worst possible outcome.

Why did my payment increase?▾

If your Note is an Adjustable Rate Mortgage (ARM), the interest rate and payment change according to its terms. Your escrow/impound account may have also increased if your insurance premium or property taxes went up — you should have received an Escrow Analysis report whenever your impound account changed.

Can I make a payment online?▾

Yes — register for portal access first (see the registration help documentation), then you can log in and make payments, or view and download billing statements, any time.

Where do I mail my payments?▾

4600 Kietzke Lane, Ste K-225, Reno, NV 89502.

My loan was recently transferred to Madison and I haven't received a statement yet — can I still pay?▾

Yes. Your Welcome Letter includes your new loan number and the correct mailing address for payments — write the new loan number on your check and mail it to the address on the Welcome Letter. Billing statements are generated and mailed 15 days before your next due date.

How do I get my past-due loan back in good standing?▾

You'll need to make all past-due payments plus any fees and interest charged as a result. Request a loan reinstatement quote from your Customer Service Representative at (877) 563-4164 — if you're unable to catch up, they may be able to set up a plan to get you back on track.

When will my past-due payment be reported to the credit agencies?▾

Madison reports to the main credit bureaus every month, so even one late payment may appear on your credit report. On-time payments build a positive credit history through the same monthly reporting. Call (877) 563-4164 with questions.
